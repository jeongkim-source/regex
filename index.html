Email Address メールアドレス

Local part (before @) must be ASCII. Internationalised domains (日本語.jp) are accepted — Keycloak converts them to punycode. Wrong-width characters — full-width Latin (ｅｘａｍｐｌｅ) and half-width katakana (ﾆﾎﾝ) — are rejected everywhere in the address.

    Active rule & regex
    Local part — ASCII letters, digits and ! # $ % & ' * + / = ? ^ _ ` { | } ~ -, in dot-separated groups. Max 64. 
    
        Domain — dot-separated labels, letters/digits/hyphen, hyphen not at either end. Unicode letters allowed (IDN). TLD ≥ 2 letters.
        
    
        Normalization — NFKC, then trim, then lowercase. Safe here because the field holds no kanji.
        Full-width Latin U+FF21-U+FF3A / U+FF41-U+FF5A and half-width katakana
        U+FF61-U+FF9F are excluded from the fold and rejected, on both sides of the
        @ — each script must be entered at its correct width.
      
    
    
    
  
    

  
  
    
    
    Name 氏名 — lastName & firstName
    
    
    The name is stored as two Keycloak attributes, lastName (family name, 姓) and firstName (given name, 名), but one rule governs both — so a value tested here is tested for both. Taken verbatim from the User Profile config, that rule accepts kanji, hiragana, full-width katakana and Latin, a space (山田 太郎), digits anywhere but the first position (太郎2), and up to 120 characters. Because \p{IsLatin} covers them, full-width Latin (Ｓｍｉｔｈ) is accepted too; half-width katakana (ﾔﾏﾀﾞ) is not.

      Furigana フリガナ — lastNameKana & firstNameKana
      
    
      The reading of the name, stored as lastNameKana (セイ) and firstNameKana (メイ). Structurally identical to the name pair above — same normalization, same 64-unit limit, and again lastNameKana may not contain a space while firstNameKana may. The character set is the difference: full-width katakana only (ヤマダ), plus ー, ヽ ヾ and ・ for foreign readings. Kanji (山田), hiragana (やまだ) and Latin (Yamada) are rejected here even though the name fields accept them — and hiragana is not folded to katakana for you.
