Email Address メールアドレス

Local part (before @) must be ASCII. Internationalised domains (日本語.jp) are accepted — Keycloak converts them to punycode.

    Active rule & regex
    Local part — ASCII letters, digits and ! # $ % & ' * + / = ? ^ _ ` { | } ~ -, in dot-separated groups. Max 64. 
    
        Domain — dot-separated labels, letters/digits/hyphen, hyphen not at either end. Unicode letters allowed (IDN). TLD ≥ 2 letters.
        
    
        Normalization — full NFKC, then trim, then lowercase. Safe here because the field holds no kanji.
